About SmartCity Hub

We are a global platform for low-carbon smart city construction, integrating technology and sustainability to reshape urban development.

Our Mission

SmartCity Hub is committed to building low-carbon, efficient, and livable smart cities through digital technology, renewable energy, and intelligent urban management.

Founded in 2015, we've partnered with 80+ city governments and tech enterprises to implement smart city solutions across 65 countries.

Our approach combines IoT, big data, and AI to solve urban challenges such as energy waste, traffic congestion, and environmental pollution.
